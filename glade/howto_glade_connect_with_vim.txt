on Vim, this:

:'<,'>s/Gtk.\(.*\) \(.*\);/\2 = (Gtk.\1) builder.GetObject ("\2");

converts this:
                Gtk.VBox vbox_encoder_mass_inertia_title;
                Gtk.Label label_encoder_exercise_mass;                                                                 
                Gtk.VBox vbox_encoder_mass_inertia;
                Gtk.HBox hbox_encoder_exercise_mass;
                Gtk.Box box_encoder_exercise_inertia;
                Gtk.Image image_extra_mass;

into:
                vbox_encoder_mass_inertia_title = (Gtk.VBox) builder.GetObject ("vbox_encoder_mass_inertia_title");
                label_encoder_exercise_mass = (Gtk.Label) builder.GetObject ("label_encoder_exercise_mass");                                                                 
                vbox_encoder_mass_inertia = (Gtk.VBox) builder.GetObject ("vbox_encoder_mass_inertia");
                hbox_encoder_exercise_mass = (Gtk.HBox) builder.GetObject ("hbox_encoder_exercise_mass");
                box_encoder_exercise_inertia = (Gtk.Box) builder.GetObject ("box_encoder_exercise_inertia");
                image_extra_mass = (Gtk.Image) builder.GetObject ("image_extra_mass");
