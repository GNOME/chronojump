Project Description: Generating 21 Tones with Musical Frequencies for the Course Navette

Project Details

This file describes how we generated the 21 tones for the Course Navette using the frequencies of the musical notes of octaves 3, 4 and 5, with the Adobe Audition software. The technical characteristics used for its creation are detailed below:

Note Frequencies:

The frequencies are based on the notes A (A) to G (G) in octaves 3, 4 and 5.

Frequency Table:

Note     | Octave 3 (Hz)| Octave 4 (Hz)| Octave 5 (Hz)
---------|--------------|--------------|--------------
A (La)   | 220          | 440          | 880          
B (Si)   | 246.94       | 493.88       | 987.76       
C (Do)   | 261.62       | 523.25       | 1046.5       
D (Re)   | 293.67       | 587.33       | 1174.66      
E (Mi)   | 329.63       | 659.26       | 1318.52      
F (Fa)   | 349.23       | 698.46       | 1396.92      
G (Sol)  | 392          | 783.99       | 1567.98 

Software Used:

Adobe Audition.

Path to Generate Tones:

Go to Effects > Generate > Tones.

Specific Settings:

Waveform: Sine (sine wave).

Frequencies: Enter the 21 frequencies from the table above.

Duration: Each tone has a duration of 1 second.

Gain: All tones are generated with a gain of 0 dB to ensure a uniform level without distortion.

Output Format:

The tones can be exported as individual files in .mp3 format.
